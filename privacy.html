Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Solomonk is a language-learning app published by Erwan Le Gloannic, an individual based in France. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and what your rights are. I am the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact: legloannicerwan@gmail.com

The short version

  • You can use Solomonk without an account. The app creates an anonymous guest profile so your progress is saved.
  • If you create an account, your email address is stored too.
  • Your data is only used to run the app. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account).

What data the app uses

Guest profile (everyone). When you first open the app, an anonymous profile is created with a random identifier. No name or email is needed.

Account (if you create one). When you save your progress, you sign in with:

  • your email address (you receive a one-time code), or
  • Sign in with Apple (Apple shares your email address, or a private relay address if you choose to hide it), or
  • Sign in with Google (Google shares your email address and, depending on your Google settings, your name and profile picture).

Your learning data. The words you save, the words you mark as focus, the text each word was saved from, your daily goal, your streak and the days you reached your goal.

Translation reports. If you report a translation, the report is stored with your identifier and the word concerned, so it can be reviewed and fixed.

Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and paid through Apple; I never see your payment details. RevenueCat, the service that manages the app's subscriptions, receives your app identifier and your purchase information from Apple (product, dates, status), so the app knows whether you have Premium.

Technical data. Like any online service, the servers that run the app and RevenueCat may record technical information such as your IP address, device model, system version and app version, to deliver the service and keep it secure.

Kept on your phone only. Your learning language, where you stopped reading, whether you finished the welcome screens, whether you rated the app, and the install date used for the launch offer. The reminder before the end of a free trial is scheduled on your phone.

Anonymous statistics. The app counts how many times each subject is viewed and read further, as simple totals per subject. These counts are not linked to you.

Emails you send. "Contact Us" and "Report Error" open your own email app with a prefilled message. I receive it like any email.

What the app doesn't do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no third-party analytics.
  • No selling or renting of your data.

Why the data is used (legal bases)

  • To provide the app and your account, save your progress and manage your subscription: this is needed to perform our agreement (the Terms of Use).
  • To review translation reports, improve the texts and keep the service secure: my legitimate interest in offering a reliable app.
  • To keep purchase records when the law requires it: legal obligation.

Who processes your data

Only these providers, and only to run the app:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Stores your profile, account and learning data.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management.
  • Apple: App Store purchases, and Sign in with Apple if you use it.
  • Google: only if you choose Sign in with Google.

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, the United States. These transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long data is kept

  • Account and learning data: as long as your account exists. Deleting your account (Profile → Delete Account) permanently deletes it with its saved words, stats and reports.
  • Guest data: until you create an account (your guest data then moves into it and the guest profile is deleted), or until you ask for it to be deleted.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple and RevenueCat as their own legal obligations require (for example accounting and tax law).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access your data, correct it, delete it, get a copy of it, and object to or limit some uses of it.

  • With an account, you can delete everything yourself in the app: Profile → Delete Account.
  • As a guest, you can create an account and then delete it, or email me.
  • For any other request, email legloannicerwan@gmail.com. I answer within one month.

If you think your rights are not respected, you can file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).

Children

Solomonk is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has used the app, contact me and the data will be deleted.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each user can only access their own data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
