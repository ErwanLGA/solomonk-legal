How do I restore my purchase?

In the app: Profile → Restore Purchase, or "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the Premium screen. It works with or without an account, using the Apple ID that made the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Solomonk. You can also go to Profile → Manage Subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or of the free trial) to avoid the next charge.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Profile → Save Progress, then sign in the same way (email, Apple or Google) on your new phone. Your words and streak come with you.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete Account (shown once you have an account). This permanently deletes your account and its data. It does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel that in your iPhone settings.

A translation looks wrong

Tap the word, then the flag in the top-right corner of the card. Reports are reviewed to fix the translation for everyone.
